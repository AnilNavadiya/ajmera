FAQs – Awards & Recognitions

Learn why our award-winning pedigree directly benefits our franchise partners.

What makes Ajmera Trends an award-winning clothing franchise?

Ajmera Trends has won over 50+ national and international awards for fabric quality, direct-from-factory pricing transparency, zero royalty franchise models, and ethical manufacturing practices.

How do these awards benefit a local franchise store?

When you open an Ajmera Trends franchise, you instantly gain customer trust. Customers recognize our brand as a proven national entity featured on national media and recognized by celebrity icons, drastically reducing local customer acquisition costs.

Are these awards verified by independent bodies?

Yes, our awards have been conferred at reputable national summits, government forums, and recognized trade councils with comprehensive auditing of our business ethics and operational scale.

Ajmera Trends is India’s premier clothing franchise brand backed by Ajmera Fashion Pvt. Ltd., offering turnkey retail solutions, direct manufacturer pricing, and zero royalty.
