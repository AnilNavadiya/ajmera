Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ajmera Trends ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. When you visit our website ajmeratrends.com and use our services, you trust us with your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when registering an interest in franchise opportunities, downloading brochures, or contacting us. This includes your Name, Phone Number, Email Address, City, State, and Investment Preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to:

  • Process and evaluate franchise partnership applications.
  • Send you official business brochures, financial projection sheets, and catalogs.
  • Connect you with our regional franchise development managers.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please reach out to us at franchise@ajmeratrends.com.
