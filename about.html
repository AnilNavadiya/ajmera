Founder's Vision

Transforming Dreams into Thriving Businesses

Ajmera Trends was established under the visionary leadership of Mr. Ajay Ajmera with a singular mission: to make premium Indian fashion accessible to every family across India while empowering aspiring entrepreneurs with high-margin, sustainable franchise stores.

Starting with humble beginnings in Surat's bustling textile markets, Mr. Ajmera built one of the nation's most formidable garment manufacturing powerhouses. Today, Ajmera Trends is bridging the gap between direct manufacturing and retail customers, eliminating predatory middlemen and passing unbeatable margins directly to our franchise partners.

Manufacturing Hub

State-of-the-Art Surat Infrastructure

Our sprawling manufacturing facilities in Surat are equipped with computerized jacquard looms, digital textile printing units, automated embroidery stations, and rigorous quality check laboratories.

With an integrated production capacity of over 50,000 garments daily, we ensure our franchise partners receive regular, unbroken supplies of fresh inventory at authentic factory-floor costs.
