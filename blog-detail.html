Top Apparel Franchise in India 2026: Investment, Margins & Setup Guide

Updated: February 2026 By Ajay Ajmera (Founder, Ajmera Trends) 6 Min Read

The Indian apparel and ethnic wear market is witnessing explosive exponential growth, propelled by the rising disposable incomes in Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, an unprecedented appetite for regional designer fashion, and year-round festive wedding celebrations.

Why Choose a Clothing Franchise Over Independent Sourcing?

Starting an independent retail shop carries heavy inventory risks, uncertain middleman markups, zero brand recall, and lack of visual merchandising know-how. When you invest in an established brand like Ajmera Trends, you immediately gain access to:

  • Zero Percent (0%) Royalty: Retain 100% of the operational net profit generated by your retail store.
  • Direct Mill-to-Store Logistics: Garments manufactured directly in Surat's advanced textile looms, guaranteeing 45% - 60% gross profit margins.
  • Complete Turnkey Setup: From 3D store architectural planning to LED fixtures, branded display racks, and billing barcode hardware.

How Much Investment is Required?

Ajmera Trends offers three flexible unit franchise models tailored to various carpet areas and investment appetites:

  1. Model A (Express Store): Requires ₹26 Lakhs all-inclusive for 600 - 800 sq.ft. Includes ₹15 Lakhs+ in curated opening inventory.
  2. Model B (Standard Family Store): Requires ₹35 Lakhs for 1,000 - 1,500 sq.ft. Complete family ethnic, bridal, and kids collection.
  3. Model C (Flagship Megastore): ₹50+ Lakhs for 2,000+ sq.ft multi-category destination store.

Summary Takeaway

Partnering with a proven manufacturer like Ajmera Trends cuts your break-even time down to 10-14 months, providing predictable cash flows and protected territorial exclusivity.

How to Get Started?

Fill out the franchise inquiry form on our website. Our business development team will schedule a one-on-one video orientation, share the complete project report, and evaluate location suitability in your city.
