Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about opening an Ajmera Trends clothing franchise? Find direct, transparent answers below.

Home / FAQs

1. What is the minimum cost to start an Ajmera Trends clothing franchise?

The minimum starting investment is ₹26 Lakhs (Model A - Express Store) for a 600 - 800 sq.ft store. This total cost includes the franchise fee, complete interior fixtures and branding, billing hardware/software, and ₹15 Lakhs+ in curated opening inventory.

2. Is there any clothing franchise opportunity under 5 lakhs?

No. To maintain authentic brand aesthetics, proper retail fixtures, standardized store layouts, and sufficient opening inventory, our starting turnkey model begins at ₹26 Lakhs. However, individuals with smaller budgets can inquire about wholesale dealership packages.

3. Does Ajmera Trends charge any monthly royalty or profit sharing?

ZERO (0%) Royalty. Ajmera Trends operates on a partner-first model. We do NOT charge any recurring royalties or revenue share. You retain 100% of the profits generated by your store.

4. What kind of profit margins can a franchise owner expect?

Because you receive merchandise directly from our manufacturing facilities in Surat with no middlemen, your gross profit margins range between 40% to 60% on retail sales.

5. How much retail space is required to open a franchise?

Space requirements depend on the chosen model:
  • Model A: 600 - 800 sq.ft (Express Store)
  • Model B: 1,000 - 1,500 sq.ft (Standard Family Store)
  • Model C: 2,000+ sq.ft (Flagship Megastore)

6. Does Ajmera Trends assist with store interior design and civil works?

Yes! Our specialized retail architecture team provides complete 2D/3D layouts, modular display racks, LED spot lighting, trial room designs, cash desk, and glowing branded outdoor signage.

7. What training and operational support will I receive?

We provide full staff recruitment guidance, on-site sales training, billing POS software configuration, customer service SOPs, and ongoing marketing assistance for local promotions and festival campaigns.

8. How long does it take from agreement signing to store grand opening?

Our proven timeline completes the entire process in just 45 Days: from site finalization and interior fit-out to stock dispatch and opening day promotions.
